Who we are

Binyan Bitoun (בניין ביטון) is a French-Israeli building company run by Elie Bitoun. It carries out renovations, general works and repairs across Central Israel: Tel Aviv-Yafo, Ramat Gan, Givatayim, Bat Yam, Rishon LeZion, Petah Tikva, Raanana and Herzliya.

As we put it on Instagram: “Drawing on our experience in France, we bring our know-how to offer you quality work, delivered on time.”

French craftsmanship in practice

  • Careful finishes: sharp edges, clean joints, invisible repairs.
  • Cleanliness: floors and furniture protected, tidy site, rooms handed back clean.
  • Punctuality: a schedule announced and kept.
  • Clear communication in French or Hebrew: you understand every step and every quote.
  • A project that suits you: “We talk it through together to create a final result that reflects you.”

Who we work for

French speakers living in Central Israel, owners living abroad who bought in Israel, and anyone looking for a demanding contractor. We renovate apartments, houses and commercial premises.
