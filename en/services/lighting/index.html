LED lighting installation

LED strips, path and façade lighting, indoor and outdoor accent lighting.

Message on WhatsAppRequest a quote

Binyan Bitoun installs indoor and outdoor LED lighting: integrated LED strips and lighting for paths, low walls and drop ceilings. Our before/after shows a dark outdoor path transformed by a continuous LED strip.

As we explain on Instagram, an LED strip can give as much light as many halogen bulbs while using far less energy. Well integrated, it highlights the architecture without being seen.

What we do

Paths and outdoors

LED strips set under a low wall or handrail for continuous, soft light.

Drop ceilings and coves

Indirect light in a drywall cove, recessed spotlights.

Kitchen and storage

Under-cabinet LEDs, lit niches and shelves.

Electrical coordination

For panel work or new circuits, we coordinate with a licensed electrician.

Frequently asked questions

Do LED strips work outdoors?

Yes, if you use outdoor-rated strips and profiles (IP rating) and make careful connections.

Do you do electrical work?

We install the lighting and coordinate with a licensed electrician for any panel work or new circuits.

Can lighting be dimmed or timed?

Yes, dimmers and timers are possible depending on the existing installation.
