Deep air-conditioner cleaning

Deep cleaning of wall-mounted AC units: coil, filters, tray and drain.

Message on WhatsAppRequest a quote

Binyan Bitoun deep-cleans wall-mounted (split) air conditioners: the unit is protected with a wash bag, the coil and parts are washed and the dirty water is collected. Our videos show the work and the water collected after one cleaning.

“An annual clean of your air conditioner helps keep good air quality for your family”, our advice on Instagram. In a climate where AC runs much of the year, dust and deposits build up fast on the coil.

What we do

Coil washing

The heat exchanger is washed to remove dust and deposits, see the before/after in our projects.

Protective wash bag

A wash bag collects the dirty water: nothing runs down your walls or floors.

Filters and casing

Cleaning filters, louvres and accessible parts.

Drain check

Checking the condensate flow to limit ceiling leaks.

Real before / after

When to call us

  • A bad smell when the AC starts
  • Airflow seems weaker
  • The unit has not been cleaned in a long time
  • Water drips from the indoor unit

Frequently asked questions

How often should an AC be cleaned?

We recommend once a year, ideally before the hot season. Filters should be cleaned more often.

Does cleaning make a mess?

No: the unit is wrapped in a wash bag that collects the dirty water and the area is protected. Our video shows it.

Do you clean ducted units too?

Contact us with the model and access to the unit, and we will tell you what can be done.
