Apartment renovation in Central Israel

Full or partial renovation of apartments and premises, managed end to end, in French, English or Hebrew.

Message on WhatsAppRequest a quote

Binyan Bitoun renovates apartments and premises in Central Israel (Tel Aviv-Yafo, Ramat Gan, Givatayim, Bat Yam, Rishon LeZion, Petah Tikva, Raanana, Herzliya). The company is run by Elie Bitoun, works with French standards of finish, and can run a project for an owner living abroad with photo and video updates on WhatsApp.

A successful renovation in Israel is mostly about coordination: walls, paint, ceilings, joinery, lighting, bathrooms. We bring these trades into one project with a single point of contact who reports back to you at every stage.

What we do

Full renovation

Complete refurbishment of an apartment or commercial space: surface preparation, plaster, paint, lighting, joinery and finishing. Our video shows a full before/after of a commercial premises.

Refresh before renting or selling

Painting, crack and damp repair, new kitchen or wardrobe fronts, the essentials to bring a property back to its best.

Small works and repairs

Odd jobs, repairs after a leak, adjustments: we also take on one-off jobs.

Remote project management

Living abroad? We handle site visits, send photos and videos at each step and confirm decisions with you on WhatsApp.

Frequently asked questions

Can you manage a renovation while I live abroad?

Yes. We send photo and video progress updates on WhatsApp and confirm every important choice with you before going ahead.

How do I get a quote?

Send a few photos and a short description on WhatsApp or by email. We will get back to you to discuss and, if needed, arrange a visit.

Do you renovate commercial premises too?

Yes, we fully renovated a commercial space (common areas, kitchenette, restrooms). The video is on this page and in our projects.
