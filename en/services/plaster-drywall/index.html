Plaster, drywall and drop ceilings

Drop ceilings, boxing, window reveals and plaster/drywall repairs.

Message on WhatsAppRequest a quote

Binyan Bitoun does plaster and drywall (“gevess”) work: drop ceilings, boxing for AC ducts, window reveals and repairs to damaged ceilings. Our photos show a rebuilt window reveal and a neatly integrated AC vent.

Drywall is everywhere in Israeli apartments: it hides AC ducts and creates niches and drop ceilings for lighting. Done well, it disappears; badly finished, it cracks at the joints. We take care over joints, corners and grille integration.

Drop ceilings and boxing

Window reveals

Grille integration

Frequently asked questions

Plaster or drywall, what's the difference?

In Israel, “gevess” (גבס) usually means plasterboard on a metal frame, used for drop ceilings and partitions. Skim plaster is for wall finishes. We do both.

Can lighting be built into a drop ceiling?

Yes, that is one of the main reasons for one: spotlights, LED coves and more. See our lighting page.

Can a water-damaged drop ceiling be repaired?

Yes. Once the leak is fixed, we replace the affected boards and redo the joints.
