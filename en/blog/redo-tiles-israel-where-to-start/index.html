What is the first thing to do before retiling in Israel?

Work out why you are doing it. A change of style is one thing. Hollow tiles, blackened grout or a complaint from the neighbour below are another, and they change the whole job.

Walk through the room with your phone. Tap tiles with the handle of a screwdriver, since a hollow sound marks a loose tile. Photograph cracks, grout, the base of the walls and the door thresholds. Those images are the starting point of your conversation with Binyan Bitoun.

How can you tell if the substrate is sound?

A tile is only as good as what it sits on. Binyan Bitoun looks for these signs before recommending anything.

What you noticeWhat it may meanLogical next step
Hollow tiles in placesFailed adhesive or tired substrateLift that area or the whole floor
A crack across several tilesMovement underneathUnderstand the cause before laying
Uneven floorIrregular screedRepair the substrate
Damp at the base of a wallWater under the tilesFind the source and check waterproofing
Puddles on the balconyNot enough slopeCorrect the slope before tiling

Every one of these is cheaper to solve before the new tile goes down than after. A new floor over a problem simply hides it for a while.

Do you need to remove the old tiles in Israel?

Sometimes. Laying over old tile avoids demolition, noise and rubble, provided the floor is sound and flat and the tiles are firmly bonded. The floor does rise a little, which can affect doors and thresholds.

In a bathroom or on a balcony, removal lets you inspect and redo the waterproofing. Binyan Bitoun looks at the substrate before recommending either route. The guide on choosing a tiling company comes back to this point.

In what order should the tiling work happen?

Tiling comes late. Get everything hidden beneath it right first, because breaking new tile later is costly. This is the order Binyan Bitoun follows.

  1. Send photos. Show the room, the problems and the thresholds on WhatsApp.
  2. Show the site. The company inspects the substrate on site or by video.
  3. Decide on removal. Full removal or laying over, depending on the real condition.
  4. Deal with what is underneath. Pipes, drains and waterproofing come before tile.
  5. Lay the tiles. The layout is agreed with you, then work moves ahead with image updates.
  6. Finish. Grout and finishes are checked with you, and the site is left clean.

Which tile should you choose once the substrate is ready?

Choose for use, not just from a photo. A bathroom floor has to limit slips, a balcony has to take sun and rain, and a living room has to handle daily traffic.

In Israel, the Standards Institution of Israel tests slip resistance under SI 2279. Ask the supplier for the product’s data, particularly for a shower or outdoor area. Binyan Bitoun can look at the reference with you before you buy.

Three habits at the tile shop

Take a sample home and view it in the room’s light. Check the format, because large tiles demand a very flat floor. Buy extra for cuts and breakage, and note the batch number so the shade stays consistent.

How do you organize tiling in an apartment building in Israel?

Removal is noisy and dusty, and rubble has to go down somehow. According to Kol Zchut, the Israeli rights information site, noisy work is banned on weekdays from 20:00 to 07:00, from 17:00 on the eve of a rest day and during the rest day. Some cities are stricter. Warn the neighbours and the Vaad Bayit.

Kol Zchut also explains that an owner may do work at home as long as it harms neither another unit nor the common areas. On a balcony visible from the street, changing the external look may need consent. Binyan Bitoun flags what it notices before work starts.

What should a tiling quote include in Israel?

Cost depends on area, substrate condition, removal and the tile. Compare on substance: removal and rubble disposal, substrate preparation, waterproofing, layout, grout, skirting and cleaning. The easiest route is to send photos to Binyan Bitoun, which prepares a quote for your room.

Also check who buys the tiles and who gets them to your floor. That detail matters in a walk-up building.
