I have a house with a garden in Raanana, who can redo the outdoor areas?

Binyan Bitoun works on Raanana houses and gardens, from path lighting to gutters, roofs and terraces. The company has 10 years of experience in construction and more than 200 satisfied clients, and Raanana is one of its 8 cities.

How is renovating in Raanana different from the coastal strip?

The city is more spacious. Wikipedia says it was founded in 1922 as an American-Jewish settlement, first called Ahuza A, New York, and that many English-speaking immigrants live there today. It describes the city as affluent and names Raanana Park as a major green space. Houses, semi-detached homes and small buildings stand alongside recent developments. As a result, work often moves beyond the apartment and into the outdoors. Binyan Bitoun deals with both.

Roof terraces

Many homes have a terrace above a living space, so any defect shows up straight away in the ceiling below. Check it before the rains.

Houses

They have needs an apartment owner does not face: a roof, a terrace, low walls, paths and gutters. Because no Vaad Bayit shares the load, every repair and every decision rests with you, so a yearly walk around the property pays off.

Gardens

Outdoor light changes how a garden is used after dark, and it makes steps and passages safer. Roots can lift paving over time, and sprinklers aimed at a wall can leave damp at its base, so walk the garden with an eye on both.

Central apartments

Around Ahuza Street, the historic main street, buildings of different periods call for kitchens, paint and upgrades.

How do you prepare the outside of a house, step by step?

A house is best handled from the top down, so water does not come back to ruin new work. Binyan Bitoun follows this logic.

  1. Begin with the roof. Check outlets, edges and coating before anything else.
  2. Fit the gutters. Rainwater has to leave the walls before facades get attention.
  3. Repair walls and low walls. Cracks and exterior paint come next.
  4. Redo the terraces. Waterproofing precedes the floor and any planting.
  5. Run the lighting. Cable routes are planned before paths are finished.
  6. End with the surroundings. Paths, steps and edging close out the job.

How do you light a garden path without glare?

Aim for atmosphere rather than power. Walk the garden at dusk and note where you actually need to see your feet. An LED strip under a low wall guides the way without dazzling anyone, and low points mark the steps. Keep cable routes away from future planting, and avoid aiming light at the neighbours’ windows, and choose outdoor-rated products, since rain and sun soon destroy indoor ones. Binyan Bitoun fits outdoor LED strips. For new circuits, ask from the start who handles the electrical side.

Does a house in Raanana need a gutter?

If water runs down the wall, yes. Dark streaks after rain, paint flaking low on a wall and broken tiles along a wall top are good clues. A gutter collects the rain and carries it away from the house. The Israel Meteorological Service reports that, in the central coastal plain, precipitation falls almost exclusively from October to May, so this work belongs in summer. Binyan Bitoun fits gutters, and roof waterproofing can happen on the same visit.

How do you renovate an older apartment near Ahuza Street?

The job resembles one in a denser city: limited access, close neighbours and shops at street level. Delivery times matter, as a busy street leaves little room for a truck during the day. Kol Zchut, the Israeli rights information site, says noisy work is banned on weekdays from 20:00 to 07:00, and some municipalities are stricter, so ask before scheduling demolition. Binyan Bitoun then works by priority: wet rooms, ceilings, kitchen and finally paint. The guide on where to start with tiles can help if the floor is part of the plan.
