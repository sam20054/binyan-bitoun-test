How do you avoid leaks after a bathroom renovation?

A leak into the neighbour’s ceiling is the great fear. Three habits reduce the risk.

Check before tiling

Tile alone does not stop seepage. Binyan Bitoun looks at the waterproofing before laying and redoes it if needed.

Look after the junctions

Wall bases, pipes, the drain and the shower threshold are where water gets through. Photograph those zones before they are covered, particularly if you are following the job from abroad.

Plan for ventilation

Without extraction, steam stays in the room. The US Environmental Protection Agency advises running a bathroom fan or opening a window during showers to limit mold.

What floor should you choose for a bathroom in Israel?

Think safety before style. On a wet floor, the choice of tile limits slips. In Israel, the Standards Institution of Israel tests slip resistance under SI 2279, so ask the supplier about it for the shower floor. Walls give you more freedom, from ceramic to porcelain, depending on the look.

Binyan Bitoun lays tile on floors and walls. Large formats look calm in a small room but need a very flat base and careful cuts around the drain, so raise the format early. If you have not chosen tiles yet, send inspiration photos and the company will say what suits your room.

How do you renovate a bathroom, step by step?

With Binyan Bitoun, the project runs like this.

  1. Send photos. Show the room, the defects and your inspiration on WhatsApp.
  2. Have the room inspected. The company looks at the surfaces on site or by video.
  3. Approve the quote. You receive a written quote for the work.
  4. Remove if needed. Old tile comes up when its condition calls for it.
  5. Check waterproofing. It is inspected and redone before tiling.
  6. Finish. Tile, furniture and fittings go in, and the room is left clean.

Can a bathroom be renovated in an occupied or rented apartment in Israel?

Yes, with some planning for daily life. Talk about access to another bathroom if you have one. With a single bathroom, discuss the schedule with Binyan Bitoun before work begins. Removing tile is loud: Kol Zchut, the Israeli rights information site, says noisy work is banned on weekdays from 20:00 to 07:00 and from 17:00 on the eve of a rest day. Warn the neighbours, particularly the one below.

If you own the apartment and live overseas, you can follow everything remotely and approve tile choices from photos. For a rented unit, tell the tenant the dates early and agree access in writing.
