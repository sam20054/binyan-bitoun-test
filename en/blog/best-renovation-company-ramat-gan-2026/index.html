What makes Ramat Gan different for a renovation?

The city really has two characters. Wikipedia notes that it was founded in 1921 as a satellite town of Tel Aviv, and that the Diamond Exchange district in the northwest now holds its skyscrapers, including one of Israel’s tallest buildings. Between those two eras sit the family buildings of the 1950s to 1970s that make up much of the housing. Work in a tower and work in a three-room walk-up have little in common, so Binyan Bitoun first asks what sort of building you live in.

Buildings from the 1950s to 1970s

Cracks, damp marks and worn-out finishes are the usual findings. The project starts with a diagnosis of what can stay and what cannot.

Recent towers

The structure is rarely the concern. People ask about finishes, lighting, storage and fit-out, and the building’s own rules often set the hours and elevator use.

After a Tama 38 project

Many buildings have gone through seismic strengthening. Handover often leaves interior touch-ups and finishing, which Binyan Bitoun can take on.

How do you plan a renovation in Ramat Gan, step by step?

With Binyan Bitoun, the plan adapts to your building.

  1. State the building type. A tower and a small older block are prepared differently, so mention it in your first message.
  2. Share the house rules. If your tower sets hours or elevator bookings, pass them along.
  3. Let the company see the apartment. A visit shows the true condition of walls, ceilings and wet rooms.
  4. Decide room by room. If you plan to stay in the apartment, the job can be staged.
  5. Review the written quote. Each item is listed so nothing is left vague.
  6. Follow along on WhatsApp. Photos and videos arrive as each room gets finished.

Can you stay in your apartment during work in Ramat Gan?

Often, yes, for a partial renovation done room by room. For heavier work with floors coming up and ceilings redone, moving out for a while makes life simpler. Binyan Bitoun talks this through before fixing the schedule.

Warn the neighbours too. Kol Zchut, the Israeli rights information site, says noisy work is banned on weekdays from 20:00 to 07:00 and from 17:00 on the eve of a rest day, and that some municipalities are stricter. Check the bylaw in force in Ramat Gan.

Should you wait for a Tama 38 project before renovating in Ramat Gan?

That depends on how far along it is. If it has been talked about for years, waiting can freeze your apartment for nothing. If it is signed and dated, keep work to the priorities now and finish after handover. Ask the Vaad Bayit, and have the documents reviewed by a legal professional. Binyan Bitoun can tell you what the building work is likely to affect, based on what it sees in your unit.

Either way, a new balcony or safe room changes the layout. Wait for the plans before ordering a custom kitchen.

How much does a renovation cost in Ramat Gan?

Cost depends on size, starting condition and materials. In a recent tower most of the budget goes on finishes, and in an older block more of it goes on preparation. Binyan Bitoun prefers to see the apartment first, then itemizes the quote. The guide on choosing a contractor will help you compare offers.
