What makes a good AC installation in Israel?

Quality shows over time, in small details that disappear once everything is closed. Ask to see these points before the ceiling goes up. Binyan Bitoun pays attention to each of them.

The condensate drain

Water from the unit has to leave without backing up. A poorly connected drain ends as a ceiling stain.

Access to the unit

With mini-central, the unit must stay reachable for servicing. Think about a hatch when the ceiling is designed.

Airflow

Vents and grilles should serve the rooms without blowing straight onto a bed or sofa. The US Department of Energy also points out that air lost through ducts can be costly, so ducts need to be well fitted and insulated.

The outdoor unit

Location matters. Kol Zchut, the Israeli rights information site, says an outdoor unit that affects a building’s uniform appearance needs approval from the residents’ assembly. Find out before installation. Near the sea, salt air also wears outdoor units faster.

How is AC installed and closed, step by step?

On a mini-central job, Binyan Bitoun moves in this order.

  1. Show the apartment. Send photos, a plan and the ceiling height on WhatsApp.
  2. Choose the system. The type depends on the rooms and the planned work.
  3. Run the ducts. The unit, ducts and drains are installed.
  4. Check before closing. You receive photos before the ceiling is closed.
  5. Close up. Ducts and the unit are boxed in gypsum, with vents or grilles.
  6. Finish. Surfaces are left ready for paint and finishes reviewed with you.

Wall unit or mini-central: which should you choose?

It mostly depends on your project. Without ceiling work, a wall unit stays simple to install room by room. During a full renovation, mini-central becomes the natural option, since ceilings are open. The Department of Energy guide describes the same logic: even cooling with ducts, zoned cooling without. The article on wall units and mini-central compares them in detail, and Binyan Bitoun can review your case from photos.

What if your ceiling is already damaged around the AC?

A stain around a grille usually points to a blocked or badly connected drain. The cause has to be fixed, then the ceiling repaired. Binyan Bitoun also replaces broken or yellowed vents and restores ceilings after a condensate leak. If you only need work on the wall or ceiling, say so in your message, as the company also does these repairs alone, including plaster and gypsum work.
