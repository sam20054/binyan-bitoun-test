Why do apartments in Israel get damp?

Israel’s climate is drier than many visitors expect, but apartments still suffer. Coastal air holds a lot of moisture. Older buildings often have flat roofs and balconies that were enclosed later. Winter rain finds the weak points, and in summer air conditioning chills surfaces so that moisture settles on them. Binyan Bitoun meets all three patterns on its projects in Central Israel.

Seepage

Water enters from outside through a roof, the balcony above, a facade or the top of a wall. The stain grows after rain, often in a corner or along a ceiling.

A leak

Water escapes from a pipe or drain. The stain grows in dry weather too, typically near a bathroom, a kitchen or an AC grille.

Condensation

Nothing is getting in. Vapor from showers, cooking or laundry settles on cold surfaces. Black dots appear in corners, behind wardrobes and around windows.

What should you not do about mold?

Do not start with a paintbrush. The US Environmental Protection Agency says not to paint or caulk over moldy surfaces, because paint is likely to peel, and it stresses that mold returns if the water problem stays. Binyan Bitoun takes the same view: paint comes last.

Do not underestimate the area. The EPA says a moldy patch under about 10 square feet, roughly 1 square meter, can usually be handled by the occupant. Beyond that, or after serious water damage, it points you toward professional guidance. Do not mix cleaning products, and ventilate as you work.

Do not close up a damp ceiling. Sealing a wet void traps the problem, and stains can reappear weeks later. Binyan Bitoun opens what is necessary to see the real condition first.

How do you treat damp, step by step?

Binyan Bitoun follows this method on damp projects.

  1. Send photos. Show the stain, the whole ceiling and the room above if you can reach it.
  2. Trace the cause. The company looks for the leak or seepage before anything else.
  3. Treat the cause. The answer may be waterproofing, a drain repair or better ventilation.
  4. Let it dry. The surface dries out before any repair, or the stain will return.
  5. Repair the surfaces. Damaged material is removed, treated and leveled.
  6. Repaint. Finishing paint goes on last, over a sound surface.

How can you limit damp day to day?

Habits help mostly with condensation, and they will not stop real seepage. Binyan Bitoun suggests these for homes with no visible leak.

Ventilate wet rooms

The EPA recommends running the bathroom fan or opening a window while showering. Do the same when cooking, and avoid drying laundry in a closed room.

Watch indoor humidity

The same guide suggests staying below 60 percent relative humidity, ideally between 30 and 50 percent. An inexpensive hygrometer will show you where you stand, and a dehumidifier can help in rooms that are hard to air.

Leave a gap behind furniture

Keep a few centimeters between wardrobes and cold outer walls, particularly in bedrooms.

Keep AC drainage clear

The EPA says drip pans should stay clean and drain lines unobstructed. Binyan Bitoun checks the drain during its AC cleaning visits.

Who pays for damp repairs in Israel?

That depends on the source. According to Kol Zchut, the Israeli rights information site, the Vaad Bayit must repair common property that could harm an apartment, and a neighbour must repair what could damage the next unit. For rentals, another Kol Zchut page says the landlord repairs defects that are not minor and did not result from unreasonable use. Binyan Bitoun can describe what it found and fixed, but check your own situation with your insurer or a legal professional. The guide on water damage and who pays goes further.
