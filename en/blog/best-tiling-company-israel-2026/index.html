When is a basic tiler not enough?

Setting tile is one skill. On many sites, though, the real problem begins underneath. That is where a renovation company like Binyan Bitoun makes a difference.

The substrate is damaged

A cracked floor or crumbling wall has to be repaired before tiling. Otherwise defects come back through the new surface and tiles lift.

Waterproofing is at fault

Tile by itself will not stop seepage. On a balcony or in a shower, a leak into the apartment below often traces back to waterproofing. Binyan Bitoun checks it before laying and redoes it if needed.

The slope is wrong

A balcony has to shed water. If puddles stay after rain, the surface must be corrected first. Binyan Bitoun checks the fall toward the drain before tiling.

Several trades are involved

A bathroom usually needs tile, plaster and paint. With Binyan Bitoun you keep one contact for everything.

Can you lay new tiles over old ones in Israel?

In some cases, yes. It works if the floor is sound and flat and the existing tiles are well bonded. You avoid demolition, with less noise, dust and rubble.

It has limits too: the floor rises by a few centimeters, which can affect doors, thresholds and skirting. In a bathroom or on a balcony, full removal is often better because it lets you check and redo the waterproofing. Binyan Bitoun looks at the substrate before recommending either option.

Do you need a registered contractor for tiling in Israel?

It depends on the job. According to the Registrar of Contractors, registration is required according to the financial scale or professional nature of the work as set by regulation.

For a large project, ask the company for its contractor number, Binyan Bitoun included, and check it in the register. Also consider the building’s rules: Kol Zchut, the Israeli rights information site, says noisy work is banned on weekdays from 20:00 to 07:00, which affects tile removal.
