Why is Herzliya not a single market?

Because two worlds sit side by side. According to Wikipedia, Herzliya was founded in 1924 as a farming community, and Herzliya Pituah was established in 1925 on former sand dunes between the sea and the coastal road. The article describes Pituah as one of the country’s most affluent districts, with embassies, executives’ homes, a marina and a high-tech park. The center stays residential, with buildings of different periods. So Binyan Bitoun does not offer a villa the same thing as a four-room apartment.

How do you maintain a villa near the sea, step by step?

A house like this works best as a program rather than a string of callouts. This is what Binyan Bitoun proposes.

  1. Take inventory. List roofs, terraces, AC units and outdoor light points.
  2. Check the roof before winter. Outlets, edges and coating get reviewed at the end of summer.
  3. Clean every AC unit. A yearly round before the hot season covers all of them.
  4. Test the outdoor lights. Salt and irrigation wear connectors and fittings.
  5. Treat rust early. Railings and gates get attention at the first traces.
  6. Keep a record. Each visit is documented with photos on WhatsApp.

What can a company do while you are away?

Plenty, provided access is clear. Binyan Bitoun can carry out work in your absence and ready the house before you return, with a photo and video report. Decide who gives access, and set out what can be decided without you. For larger choices, the company confirms with you on WhatsApp.

In a second home, the enemy is unseen water. A small leak can run for weeks. Shut the main valve when you leave for long periods if the house allows it, and give a neighbour or manager a number to call. The US Environmental Protection Agency advises drying wet areas within 24 to 48 hours to prevent mold, which is hard to manage without visits. Have someone stop by before you arrive.

What finish should you expect from a Herzliya Pituah renovation?

Expectations are high, so state them plainly. Talk about alignment, joints, how AC grilles are integrated, and how ceilings run continuously. Ask for exact material references in the quote, not just the word "premium", which means nothing by itself. Before a ceiling closes, ask for photos of the ducts. Binyan Bitoun sends such images during the work. The guide on wall units and mini-central helps if cooling is up for review.

For a villa, also check local rules before touching a facade, a fence or an extension, and take an architect’s advice. Those topics go beyond finishes. Ask how pool water, irrigation and salt will affect the outdoor surfaces you choose, since those three wear finishes faster here than almost anything else. In a large house, also agree who holds the master list of products and paint codes, as it makes later touch-ups much simpler.

How do you renovate an apartment in central Herzliya?

Here the job resembles one in neighbouring cities, with a building to notify and neighbours close by. Kol Zchut, the Israeli rights information site, says work affecting common areas or the external look needs consent. The rest is yours to decide.
