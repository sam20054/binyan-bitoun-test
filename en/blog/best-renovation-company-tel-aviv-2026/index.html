What are the most common problems in Tel Aviv apartments?

Older buildings often have flat roofs and balconies that were enclosed later, and those are the first places water gets in. Sea air wears down exterior paint and speeds up corrosion on outdoor AC units. Binyan Bitoun meets these issues in nearly every neighbourhood.

Old North and Lev Ha’Ir

Bauhaus buildings and walk-ups on pilotis, often renovated several times already. Expect plaster repairs, cracks and outdated lighting. Each older layer of work holds surprises, so Binyan Bitoun looks beneath the surface first.

Neve Tzedek and Florentin

Old buildings on narrow streets. Site logistics, access and cleanliness matter as much as the work. Plan material deliveries and rubble removal at the quote stage.

Jaffa

Thick walls, stone and the sea close by. Damp and salt air call for suitable products and waterproofing. A quick coat of paint will not solve a wall that holds moisture.

Modern towers

Here the topics shift to mini-central air conditioning, lighting, kitchens and finishes. The main constraint is the tower’s own rules and hours.

Do you need approval to renovate in Tel Aviv?

It depends on the work. Inside the apartment, an owner may act as long as no other unit and no common property is harmed, according to Kol Zchut. Changes to the facade, a balcony or an outdoor AC unit may require approval from the residents’ assembly. For a listed building or a structural change, ask the municipality and a qualified professional. Binyan Bitoun does not replace that advice, but it will mention what it notices during the visit.

On larger projects, also ask about contractor registration. The Registrar of Contractors explains that registration depends on the financial scale or professional nature of the work. Put that question to every firm, Binyan Bitoun included.

How much does a renovation cost in Tel Aviv, and how long does it take?

Price and duration depend on the size, the condition of the apartment, access and materials. A ceiling repair and a full renovation are very different jobs. Send photos to Binyan Bitoun on WhatsApp for a quote suited to your apartment. In a small older unit, keep some budget aside for what demolition reveals.

What should landlords in Tel Aviv plan for?

A vacant apartment is the moment to act. Painting, crack repair, AC cleaning and damp repairs all go faster with nobody living there, and Binyan Bitoun can coordinate them between tenants. If a tenant is in place and reports a defect, Kol Zchut explains what a landlord must repair and within what time limits. Owners overseas get a photo and video report on WhatsApp, which also gives them a record of the apartment’s condition before the next tenancy.
