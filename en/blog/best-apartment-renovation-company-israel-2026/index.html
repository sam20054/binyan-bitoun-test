What order should an apartment renovation follow?

The usual mistake is to begin with what shows. The right order starts with what stays hidden. Here is the sequence Binyan Bitoun uses, adjusted to each apartment.

OrderItemWhy at this point
1Demolition and removalIt reveals the real condition of the apartment
2Leaks and waterproofingNothing gets closed over a water problem
3Services and ductsThey run before ceilings and walls close
4Plaster and gypsumCeilings and boxing are closed
5TilingIt follows the messiest stages
6Kitchen and wardrobesThey go onto clean surfaces
7Paint and finishesLast coat, last touch-ups

Following this order avoids doing the same job twice. If the ceiling will house a mini-central unit, raise it at the start so Binyan Bitoun can plan installation and closing with everything else.

How should you set a renovation budget in Israel?

Cost depends on size, starting condition, materials and finish level. Binyan Bitoun does not give a price per square meter without seeing the apartment, because two units of equal size can need very different work.

Make three lists

Essential, nice to have, and can wait. If a quote runs over, you will know what to cut without undoing the plan.

Keep a reserve

Older apartments hide surprises behind walls. Set money aside, and ask how extra work will be approved, especially if you are abroad.

Compare on substance

Check that each quote covers demolition, removal, preparation and finishes. The guide on choosing a contractor has a full checklist.

What rules apply when renovating in a shared building in Israel?

What you can decide alone

According to Kol Zchut, the Israeli rights information site, an owner may do work in their apartment if it does not harm another unit or the common property. If it does, consent is needed from the other owner or the residents’ assembly.

Working hours

National rules ban noisy renovation on weekdays from 20:00 to 07:00 and from 17:00 on the eve of a rest day, says Kol Zchut, and some cities are stricter. Binyan Bitoun plans the noisiest tasks accordingly.

Contractor registration

Check this on a sizable project. The Registrar of Contractors says registration depends on the financial scale or professional nature of the work. Ask for the number, Binyan Bitoun included.

How does an older apartment differ from a newer one?

In an older apartment, the job starts with a diagnosis. Hollow plaster, past leaks and hidden ducts all come to light, and Binyan Bitoun decides what can stay and what has to go. In a newer apartment, the focus is usually finishes, kitchen, lighting and storage.

If your apartment was delivered recently and has a defect, check your rights before repairing it. Kol Zchut sets out the periods during which a seller must correct certain defects, which vary by type of defect and contract date.
