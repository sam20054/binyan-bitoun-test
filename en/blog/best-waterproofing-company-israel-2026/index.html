When does waterproofing need redoing in Israel?

Climate explains a great deal. After a dry, hot summer, rain arrives in a few strong spells. The Israel Meteorological Service notes that at Ben Gurion Airport, in the central coastal plain, precipitation occurs almost exclusively from October to May. Add salty air near the coast and flat roofs, and waterproofing becomes an apartment’s main defense. Binyan Bitoun suggests watching for these signs.

Inside your apartment

A stain on a top-floor ceiling, paint blistering along a wall, a cracked or blackened balcony coating, or tiles lifting.

At the neighbour’s

If the neighbour below reports a stain under your balcony, act promptly. Kol Zchut, the Israeli rights information site, says each owner must repair in their own apartment what could harm another unit.

On the roof

Water sitting after rain, detached edges, clogged drains and damaged wall tops.

You can also ask how the work will be tested. Israeli standard SI 1476 part 1 describes watertightness tests by flooding for flat roofs and open balconies. Depending on the job, ask whether a test is feasible and on what terms.

How does a balcony differ from a roof in Israel?

Balconies

The area is small but full of junctions: door threshold, railing, floor and wall. Waterproofing often sits under tile, so you need to decide whether the floor has to come up. Binyan Bitoun checks the condition and slope, and tile can be relaid afterwards. In some cases the waterproof coating itself serves as the finish, as on the balcony shown above.

Flat roofs

The area is larger, and the weak points are drains, upstands and equipment supports. Israeli standard SI 1752 part 1 covers the substrate for waterproofing concrete flat roofs, a reminder that the base matters. On a shared roof, the project goes through the Vaad Bayit.

Walls

A damaged wall top lets water in. A gutter collects rain at the top of the wall and carries it away. Look at the wall after a rainy day: dark streaks running downward show where the water travels, and photos of those streaks help Binyan Bitoun decide where a gutter belongs.

Who pays for balcony or roof waterproofing in Israel?

It depends on the situation and the building’s bylaws. For a shared roof, Kol Zchut says the Vaad Bayit must act where failing to repair could harm an apartment. A balcony may be treated differently, so check with the Vaad Bayit and, if in doubt, a legal professional. Binyan Bitoun can quote the building as well as an individual owner.

In a newer building, the seller may still be involved. Kol Zchut gives a 4-year period for waterproofing failures, for contracts signed from April 6, 2011. Check your case before starting work.
