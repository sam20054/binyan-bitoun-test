In a shared building, the Vaad Bayit answers for common property and each owner for their own unit. Payment then depends on insurance. What should you do first after water damage in Israel?

Treat the first hour as damage control. Close the apartment’s main water valve if the leak is yours. If water has reached ceiling lights or sockets, switch off that circuit at the breaker. Move furniture away. Then film and photograph everything before you clean up.

Those images are useful to you, your neighbour, your insurer and the company doing the repair. Binyan Bitoun asks for them on WhatsApp to get a first read on the job.

Tell the right people

If water is coming from upstairs, contact the neighbour directly. If the source looks like the roof, a shared pipe or the stairwell, contact the Vaad Bayit, the building committee. If you rent, notify the landlord in writing. A dated WhatsApp message is a simple record.

Start drying

Open windows on dry days and move wet items out. The longer a surface stays wet, the more likely mold becomes. Avoid repainting for now: Binyan Bitoun waits until the leak has stopped and the surface is dry before touching a ceiling.

Who is responsible for repairs in a shared building in Israel?

The starting point is property law. Kol Zchut, the Israeli rights information site, explains that the Vaad Bayit must carry out repairs in the building and common areas where failing to do so could harm an apartment. Each owner must likewise repair their own apartment where neglect could harm another. That is why Binyan Bitoun starts by locating the source.

What if a neighbour or the Vaad Bayit refuses to act?

The same Kol Zchut page says the affected owner may apply to the Land Registry Supervisor, who can order the repair. An owner who paid for the repair can ask a court for reimbursement, which may be granted in certain cases and not necessarily in full.

What if the apartment is rented?

A landlord must repair at their own cost any defect that is not minor and did not come from unreasonable use. According to Kol Zchut, the limit is 30 days, or 3 days for an urgent repair, meaning a defect that prevents reasonable living in the apartment. Owners abroad can ask Binyan Bitoun to check the apartment and send images on WhatsApp.

Does home insurance cover water damage in Israel?

It depends on your policy. A 2016 circular from the Capital Market Authority, the Israeli insurance regulator, says water damage cover is provided through an extension the insured may purchase. It also says that since 2015 the extension includes damage originating with a third party. Read your own policy, and ask your insurer or agent before drawing conclusions.

Check which part of the policy applies too. The official home insurance calculator separates structure cover, for the apartment and its fixtures, from contents cover, for the belongings inside. A ceiling and a sofa may fall under different sections. Binyan Bitoun does not manage insurance claims, but it can provide before/after photos and a description of the work.

How should you handle water damage, step by step?

With Binyan Bitoun, the job runs in this order so that nothing is repaired twice.

  1. Stop the water. Close the valve if the leak is yours, and alert the neighbour or Vaad Bayit if it is not.
  2. Record the damage. Photograph ceilings, walls, floors and damaged belongings, with dates.
  3. Call your insurer. Ask what is covered and what evidence is needed before repairs begin.
  4. Find the source. Binyan Bitoun looks for the leak first so the problem is treated at its origin.
  5. Let it dry. The surface must dry once the leak stops, or stains will return.
  6. Restore. Damaged material is removed, then plaster and paint are redone.

Can you reduce a water bill after a leak in Israel?

Often, yes. Kol Zchut explains that after fixing a leak you can ask the municipal water corporation for a reduced rate if consumption reached at least one and a half times your usual level. The request must be filed within 6 months of the end of the billing period. The page also splits responsibility: the consumer for private pipes, and all residents for the section between the main meter and the apartment meter. Keep your proof of repair, whether from a plumber or from Binyan Bitoun.
