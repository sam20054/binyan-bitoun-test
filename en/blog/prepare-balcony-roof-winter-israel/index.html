Binyan Bitoun checks balconies and roofs, restores waterproofing and fits gutters in Central Israel. The company has 10 years of experience in construction and more than 200 satisfied clients.

Why prepare your balcony and roof before winter in Israel?

Because rain tends to arrive all at once after months of nothing. The Israel Meteorological Service reports that at Ben Gurion Airport, in the central coastal plain, precipitation falls almost exclusively from October to May, most often in December, January and February.

All summer, sun and heat wear down coatings while dust collects in drains. The first real storm exposes everything, often in the ceiling of the neighbour below. Binyan Bitoun sees this happen year after year in Central Israel’s flat-roofed buildings with open balconies. For overseas owners the stakes are higher, since nobody is there to notice the first drip.

What should you check on a balcony before winter?

Pick a dry day and give it half an hour. Binyan Bitoun looks at the same points during a visit.

Water should leave the balcony by itself. If it sits, something is wrong with the slope or the drain. Binyan Bitoun checks this before laying tile, because a new floor on a bad slope only hides the problem.

What should you check on a roof before winter?

A flat roof ages quietly. Go up only if access is safe and permitted, or ask for a visit instead. Binyan Bitoun treats flat roofs, upstands and sensitive points such as drains and corners.

Drains

A blocked outlet turns a roof into a basin. Clear leaves, bags and debris before the rain.

Joints and corners

Water rarely passes through the middle of a sound roof. It comes in at junctions: wall bases, pipe penetrations and supports for water heaters or AC units.

Standing water

If water stays for days after rain, the roof lacks slope or drainage. Israeli standard SI 1476 part 1 covers watertightness tests by flooding for flat roofs and open balconies, and SI 1752 part 1 deals with the substrate for waterproofing concrete flat roofs. Binyan Bitoun’s point is simple: a good roof starts underneath the product.

Gutters and wall tops

On a brick wall or damaged wall top, rain runs down and eventually gets in. A gutter catches that water and carries it away. Binyan Bitoun fits them to prevent seepage.

How do you get ready for winter, step by step?

This is the sequence Binyan Bitoun suggests, starting at the end of summer.

  1. Walk around. Note cracks, puddles, slow drains and ceiling stains below.
  2. Clean up. Remove leaves, sand and deposits, then wash and see where water stays.
  3. Send photos. Binyan Bitoun receives them on WhatsApp and prepares the visit.
  4. Prepare the surface. The surface is cleaned and cracks are repaired.
  5. Restore waterproofing. The coating goes on over a dry surface, including edges and sensitive points.
  6. Finish. The floor is relaid if needed and gutters are fitted.

When is the best time for waterproofing in Israel?

The dry season, when surfaces are dry and products cure properly. Waiting for the first leak means working between showers on a damp surface. Binyan Bitoun suggests acting as soon as you spot a problem, rather than leaving it for the autumn rush.

If winter has already started, some things still help. Clearing drains is worthwhile at any time. For the rest, photograph the problem and ask Binyan Bitoun what can be done between rainy spells. A repair on a wet surface does not last.

Who decides on roof work in a shared building in Israel?

The roof often counts as common property, but check your building’s bylaws. According to Kol Zchut, the Israeli rights information site, the Vaad Bayit must carry out repairs in the building and common areas where neglect could harm an apartment. A private balcony may be treated differently. Binyan Bitoun can quote the building as well as an individual owner. How costs are shared depends on the building, so ask the Vaad Bayit, and a legal professional if there is a dispute.
