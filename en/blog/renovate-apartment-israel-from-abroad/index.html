Can you really renovate an apartment in Israel without being there?

Yes, provided you replace your physical presence with a system. Four things normally depend on the owner being around: access, decisions, purchases and checking the work. An overseas renovation works when each of these has a clear answer before the first day on site. Binyan Bitoun runs projects for owners who live abroad, with the work on the ground and the decisions on WhatsApp.

Remote projects rarely fail on technique. They stall because a question went unanswered during a time difference, the keys were with the wrong person, or the neighbours heard about the work too late. Those are planning problems, and planning is free.

What should overseas owners prepare before work starts?

Define the goal

Are you preparing to rent out, sell, move in or host family for the holidays? The answer drives materials, finish level and timing. Say it in your first message to Binyan Bitoun.

Collect the paperwork

Have the floor plan, the Vaad Bayit contact, and any lease to hand. If a tenant is living there, agree access dates in writing.

Settle who holds the keys

Decide how the keys reach the contractor, and name a local contact if you have one. If a relative or property manager will speak for you, introduce them at the start so that nobody receives two sets of instructions.

Check the building rules

Under the model bylaws described by Kol Zchut, the Israeli rights information site, an owner may carry out work inside an apartment if it does not harm another unit or the common property. Work that affects them needs consent from the other owner or the residents’ assembly. External appearance counts, so raise anything visible from outside with the Vaad Bayit first.

How do you keep control of the project from overseas?

Agree on what you will see and when. This is the rhythm Binyan Bitoun follows, and a fair thing to ask of any contractor.

The most important line is the fourth. Once a ceiling is closed or a floor is tiled, you can no longer see what lies beneath. Photos at that moment are your record.

Use one thread

Keep project decisions in a single WhatsApp conversation. It gives you a dated history and stops details from getting lost in calls.

Agree a reply window

Work does not pause politely for a time zone. Tell Binyan Bitoun when you can answer, or name someone who can decide when you are asleep.

How does a remote renovation work, step by step?

Here is the usual path with Binyan Bitoun for an owner living abroad.

  1. Send your project. Share photos, the address and your goal on WhatsApp.
  2. Discuss the scope. Talk through what you want and what must stay.
  3. Arrange the visit. The company sees the apartment and sends you what it finds.
  4. Approve the quote. Read the written quote and ask your questions before you commit.
  5. Follow the work. Photos and videos arrive on WhatsApp, and key choices are confirmed with you.
  6. Take delivery. The finish is checked, and you receive images of the completed apartment.

How do you choose materials without visiting the store?

Start with inspiration photos, which Binyan Bitoun can use as a reference. Ask for samples to be photographed inside the actual room, in daylight, because tiles and paint change under different light. Then put exact product references in the quote so nothing is swapped by accident.

Check function as well as looks. For shower floors and balconies, slip resistance matters. The Standards Institution of Israel tests ceramic tiles for slip under SI 2279, so ask the supplier for that information before approving a tile from a photo.

What are the common mistakes when renovating from abroad?

Leaving the deadline too tight

A holiday arrival or a tenant start date leaves no slack. Share your date with Binyan Bitoun at the first conversation.

Forgetting noise rules

According to Kol Zchut, noisy renovation work is banned on weekdays between 20:00 and 07:00, from 17:00 on the eve of a rest day and during the rest day itself. Some municipalities set stricter hours. Warn neighbours early, because a dispute is much harder to calm down from another country.

Skipping the checks

On a large project, ask for the contractor registration number and check it. The Registrar of Contractors explains that registration depends on the financial scale or professional nature of the work. Ask Binyan Bitoun the same question you ask anyone else.

What happens if something goes wrong while you are away?

Leaks are the classic overseas emergency. Binyan Bitoun can inspect the damage, send you photos and video on WhatsApp and arrange the repair with you. If the apartment is rented, Kol Zchut explains what the landlord has to repair and how quickly. The guide on water damage and who pays covers the first steps and the role of the Vaad Bayit.
