Where does water damage come from in Israel?

The causes repeat from one building to the next. Binyan Bitoun finds them most often in Central Israel’s flat-roofed buildings with open balconies.

The roof or the balcony above

Worn waterproofing lets water through at the first proper rainfall. The stain shows up on the top-floor ceiling or at the neighbour’s below.

A pipe

It may be buried in a wall or above a dropped ceiling. This kind of leak grows slowly and does not need rain.

The AC drain

A blocked or badly connected condensate line lets water run around the grille. It is a frequent problem with mini-central systems.

Water running down a wall

On a damaged wall top, rain runs down the facade and finds a way in. A gutter can solve this.

How is water damage repaired, step by step?

This is how a Binyan Bitoun repair runs.

  1. Send photos. Show the stain, the full ceiling and the area above if you can reach it.
  2. Have the leak traced. The company looks for the origin so it can treat the source.
  3. Treat the cause. Waterproofing, a drain or a gutter, depending on what was found.
  4. Let it dry. The surface needs to dry out, or stains will return.
  5. Strip and rebuild. Damaged plaster and paint are removed and surfaces rebuilt.
  6. Finish. Finishing paint goes on last, blended into the surrounding area.

Should a ceiling dry before it is repaired?

Yes. Painting a damp ceiling brings the stains back. The US Environmental Protection Agency agrees in substance: it says not to paint over moldy surfaces, and that cleanup is not finished until the water problem is fixed. Drying time depends on the size of the leak, the season and ventilation. Binyan Bitoun will tell you when repairs can begin.

Meanwhile, air the room, remove anything wet and avoid trapping moisture behind furniture or a closed ceiling. Resist the urge to fill or cover a crack that appeared with the leak, since it may help show how far the water traveled. The guide on damp and mold has more on this.

Who pays for water damage in Israel?

That depends on the source and your contracts. Kol Zchut, the Israeli rights information site, says the Vaad Bayit must repair common property that could harm an apartment, and each owner must repair what could harm another unit. On insurance, the Capital Market Authority says water damage cover comes through an extension the insured may purchase, so check your policy with your insurer. Binyan Bitoun does not decide these questions or manage claims, but the guide on water damage and who pays can help you find your way.
