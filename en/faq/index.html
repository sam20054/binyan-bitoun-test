Your questions, answered

Who is Binyan Bitoun?

Binyan Bitoun (בניין ביטון) is a French-Israeli renovation and building company run by Elie Bitoun. It works in Central Israel: Tel Aviv-Yafo, Ramat Gan, Givatayim, Bat Yam, Rishon LeZion, Petah Tikva, Raanana and Herzliya, and has more than 200 satisfied clients. Its motto: “Le savoir-faire français pour vos rénovations en Israël”, French craftsmanship for your renovations in Israel.

Which languages do you speak?

Elie Bitoun and the company work in French and Hebrew: quotes, technical explanations and project updates can all be in French.

Which cities do you cover?

Only Central Israel: Tel Aviv-Yafo, Ramat Gan, Givatayim, Bat Yam, Rishon LeZion, Petah Tikva, Raanana and Herzliya.

Can you manage my project while I live abroad?

Yes. We arrange visits, send photo and video progress updates on WhatsApp and confirm every important choice with you.

What kind of work do you do?

Apartment and commercial renovation, water damage and damp repair, painting and cracks, plaster and drop ceilings, AC integration and cleaning, kitchens and wardrobes, LED lighting, balcony and roof waterproofing, bathrooms and WCs, custom fitting.

Do you take on small jobs and repairs?

Yes. Alongside renovations, we do odd jobs and repairs.

How do I get a quote?

What does “French craftsmanship” mean to you?

Water damage & damp

Should the ceiling dry before repair?

Yes. The leak must be stopped and the surface left to dry. Painting a damp ceiling brings the stains back. We will tell you when repairs can start.

Can you help with insurance or the neighbour?

We can provide before/after photos and a description of the work, which are useful for your claim. The claim itself remains your responsibility.

Can you act if I am abroad when it happens?

Yes. We can inspect the damage, send you photos and videos on WhatsApp and organise the repair with you remotely.

Painting & crack repair

Is a crack serious?

Not necessarily, but it is worth a look: a thin plaster crack is easy to fix, while one that widens or returns may point to a deeper issue. Send us a photo.

Do you protect furniture and floors?

Yes. A clean site is part of how we work: protection, daily clean-up and rooms handed back clean.

Can I stay in the apartment while you paint?

Often yes, by working room by room. We plan it with you depending on the project.

Plaster & drywall ceilings

Plaster or drywall, what's the difference?

In Israel, “gevess” (גבס) usually means plasterboard on a metal frame, used for drop ceilings and partitions. Skim plaster is for wall finishes. We do both.

Can lighting be built into a drop ceiling?

Yes, that is one of the main reasons for one: spotlights, LED coves and more. See our lighting page.

Can a water-damaged drop ceiling be repaired?

Yes. Once the leak is fixed, we replace the affected boards and redo the joints.

Air conditioning: vents & integration

Do you install new AC units?

We mainly work on integration (vents, grilles, boxing) and maintenance. For a full installation project, contact us and we will tell you what we can take on.

Why is my ceiling stained around the AC?

Usually a blocked or badly connected condensate drain. The cause must be fixed first, then the ceiling repaired.

What is a linear grille?

A slim, elongated supply vent, more discreet than a standard grille. Our project #5 shows one.

AC cleaning

How often should an AC be cleaned?

We recommend once a year, ideally before the hot season. Filters should be cleaned more often.

Does cleaning make a mess?

No: the unit is wrapped in a wash bag that collects the dirty water and the area is protected. Our video shows it.

Do you clean ducted units too?

Contact us with the model and access to the unit, and we will tell you what can be done.

Kitchens & wardrobes

Do I need a whole new kitchen?

Not always. If the carcasses are sound, new fronts and handles often transform the room, as in our before/after.

Do you do custom joinery?

How long does a kitchen makeover take?

It depends on the scope. We include a schedule in the quote.

LED lighting

Do LED strips work outdoors?

Yes, if you use outdoor-rated strips and profiles (IP rating) and make careful connections.

Do you do electrical work?

We install the lighting and coordinate with a licensed electrician for any panel work or new circuits.

Can lighting be dimmed or timed?

Yes, dimmers and timers are possible depending on the existing installation.

Balcony & roof waterproofing

When should waterproofing be done?

Ideally in the dry season, before the first rains, so the surface is dry and products cure properly.

Who pays for roof waterproofing in a building?

It depends on the situation and the building’s rules (Vaad Bayit). We can quote for the building committee or for a private owner.

What is a coping?

A cap over the top of a wall that stops water getting in. Our before/after #6 shows one.
