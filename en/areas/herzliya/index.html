Apartment and villa renovation in Herzliya

Herzliya Pituah, centre, Neve Amirim

Message on WhatsAppRequest a quote

Binyan Bitoun works in Herzliya, from Herzliya Pituah, a villa district popular with French families, to the city centre. Frequent jobs: roof and terrace waterproofing, outdoor lighting, AC maintenance and high-end interior renovation.

Herzliya combines a residential centre with Herzliya Pituah, a seaside villa district home to an affluent French-speaking community, often split between France and Israel. Expectations on finish are high.

Neighbourhoods we cover

Herzliya Pituah

Villas and large houses near the sea: waterproofing, exterior lighting, AC, high-end finishes.

City centre

Residential buildings of various eras: apartment renovation, painting, kitchens.

Neve Amirim and residential areas

Houses and low-rise buildings: exteriors, wet rooms, storage.

Buildings and typical issues

Seaside villas combine many challenges: flat roofs, multiple AC units, exterior lighting and salty air. Regular maintenance stops small problems becoming big damage.

French-speaking and overseas owners

For second-home owners in Herzliya Pituah, we carry out work while you are away with photo and video reports, and prepare the house before you return.
